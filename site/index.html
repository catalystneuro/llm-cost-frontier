LLM Cost Frontier

The cheapest way to reach each level of LLM capability, and how fast that cost is falling. Refreshed four times a day from Artificial Analysis.

This page keeps the figures from What Happens When the Cost of Intelligence Drops 100x current. The data is Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index and their measured cost per Intelligence Index task (the average billed cost, including reasoning tokens, to run one task from their evaluation suite), pulled automatically four times a day by the llm-cost-frontier pipeline. Every observed price is kept, and models that drop out of their live benchmarking keep their history here, so the record only grows. Data as of …, covering … models.

The Pareto Frontier Over Time

Intelligence against measured cost per task, with the Pareto frontier (the cheapest model at each intelligence level) reconstructed from model release dates and observed prices as it stood on the first of every second month, and as it stands today. The Intelligence Index is a broad aggregate; the tabs above switch the vertical axis to a single evaluation that maps more directly onto a kind of application. The Play button animates the chart, building up one frontier at a time. Hover any point for the model behind it.

Recent Frontier Advances

Each entry is a date on which a model became the cheapest way to reach some level of the Intelligence Index, through a release or a price change. Subscribe to the Atom feed to be notified of new ones.

The method behind every number on this page, its limitations, and the handling of index recompositions are described on the methodology page. The updater, the observation history, and this page's data live in the llm-cost-frontier repository.
